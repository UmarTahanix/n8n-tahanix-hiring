This is an n8n workflow that automates the hiring process for automation role in a startup named Tahanix.
It is based on a webhook in a Google Form that once the form is filled, the info is sent to the workflow, and processed.
If the person who filled the application has the requirements, his details (Name, Email, Phone Number, LinkedIn URL and Github portfolio URL) are sent to the Telegram Bot, and an email message is sent automatically to the applicant, informing them of being shortlisted and that they get ready for the next step in the hiring process.
Otherwise, if they lack in any requirement, they receive an email to get informed that they are not suitable enough for the position, and not to give up, keep developing their skills and seek other opportunities.
